# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Sri Lankan business owners evaluating website design, online ordering, payments, or a custom digital system for their business.

## Product Purpose

Explain Zyrix’s website services and prices, show interactive examples, and let visitors request a free website plan or contact the studio directly.

## Positioning

Zyrix publishes four package prices and compares each with its corresponding market benchmark. The site explains the package inclusions, delivery times, ownership, and payment split up front.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The homepage contains service and portfolio demos, a pricing comparison, FAQs, and a free-plan enquiry form.
- Visitors can also reach the studio via WhatsApp or email.
- Keep the documented package prices, delivery terms, contact details, and URLs consistent with the product copy in CONTENT.md.

## Evidence on Hand

CONTENT.md records the current copy and offer. The portfolio contains interactive examples of restaurant ordering, a product shop, and a corporate site.
